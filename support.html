Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the App, go to Profile, and tap the subscription section. Your subscription will automatically restore if you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I sync my data across devices?

iCloud sync is a premium feature. Ensure you're signed in with the same Apple ID on all devices, and your training data will sync automatically via iCloud.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile > Side Menu > Settings > Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data from our servers. Local data can be removed by deleting the app.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your training data is stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud container (premium). Social features are optional and have granular privacy controls. See our Privacy Policy for details.
