About

Senior engineering expertise across enterprise infrastructure and modern application development

Super Core Solutions LLC was founded by Don Super, a senior systems engineer with over 12 years of experience in enterprise IT infrastructure. Starting at PPG Industries and progressing through Westinghouse Electric, American Eagle Outfitters, and Vail Resorts, Don developed deep specialization in identity platforms, cloud automation, and the integrations that connect them.

That specialization spans Active Directory, Entra ID, Okta, OneLogin, and Ping Identity, along with the automations that tie these systems together — HR-to-identity lifecycle workflows, iPaaS development with Workato, PowerShell scripting, and Azure Infrastructure as Code with Terraform.

Super Core Solutions was established to offer this specialized expertise to organizations that need senior-level identity and infrastructure talent without a full-time hire. Whether it's a one-time migration, an ongoing automation build, or an identity platform integration, the goal is the same: infrastructure that runs itself.

The company has since extended into application development, building and shipping production mobile applications. This isn't a separate skill set — it's the same engineering discipline applied to a different domain. Apps are built on the same foundations that enterprise systems demand: proper authentication, secure API integrations, scalable cloud backends, and infrastructure that's deployed and monitored correctly.
