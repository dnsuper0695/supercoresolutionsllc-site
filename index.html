Train BJJ — Jiujitsu Tracker

Train BJJ is a comprehensive training companion for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu athletes. Track sessions, log techniques, analyze your game with AI-powered insights, and connect with training partners — all in one app.

Features include live session tracking with Dynamic Island, a library of 150+ techniques, AI coaching, HealthKit integration, shareable session cards, and cross-device sync via iCloud.
